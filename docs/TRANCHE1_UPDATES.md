# Tranche 1 Updates

Highlights of the SCF #45 Tranche 1 work. The design is in the [ARCHITECTURE docs](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Anchor Platform

- SAP 5.0.0 serves SEP-1, 10, 12, 6 and 24 on testnet. Its payment observer watches the distribution account.
- The prototype SEP handlers in this repo were removed. The repo is now the Business Server behind SAP.
- Deployed on Railway: SAP at `anchor.slimepay.com`, the Business Server, and Postgres. `deploy/` has the same stack for a single machine.

## KYC

- SAP's customer callback is backed by Dojah: BVN or NIN checked against name and date of birth, then sanctions and PEP screening.
- A possible screening match goes to manual review. A confirmed match is rejected.
- Customer data is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). The audit log records decisions without personal data.

## Withdrawals

- SEP-24 withdrawal page: identity, bank account, amount and naira quote, then `request_onchain_funds`.
- SEP-6 withdrawals start automatically once the customer has passed KYC.
- The processor checks each payment on Horizon before paying out, then reports `notify_offchain_funds_pending` and `notify_offchain_funds_sent`. Failures go through `notify_transaction_error`.

## Payouts

- Slimepay core has an internal anchor payout API with its own SafeHaven credentials.
- It only runs against the SafeHaven sandbox. Production URLs are refused.
- One payout per transaction, enforced by a unique index and a conditional claim, so retries and crashes never pay twice.

## Reconciliation

- Before payout: any difference between SAP, the ledger and the quote puts the transaction on hold with an alert.
- After completion: SAP, the ledger and core are compared for every withdrawal, and payments to the distribution account that match no withdrawal are flagged.

## SEP-10 and stellar.toml

- SEP-10 is handled by SAP. The earlier fixes to the prototype (single use challenges, signer thresholds, `web_auth_domain`) now come from SAP itself.
- stellar.toml is generated by the Business Server, served by SAP and proxied on slimepay.com. Assets are marked `test`.
